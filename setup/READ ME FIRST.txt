ULTRA EDIT - edit videos with AI in YOUR editor (Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, After Effects)

What it does: give your AI assistant a talking-head or screen recording (and optionally a separate voice
file). It transcribes it, finds the hooks and best parts, proposes a plan, waits for your OK, then builds a
layered edit in YOUR editor: cuts, captions that follow the voice, b-roll, zooms and highlights when you ask,
sound effects, music ducking, and an export. It never uploads or posts anything.

Works with:
- Premiere Pro (Windows)        - connector included: connectors/ultra-premiere-mcp
- After Effects                 - through the same Premiere panel, or the open-source After Effects MCP
- DaVinci Resolve free / Studio - open-source davinci-resolve-mcp (Studio: also connectors/ultra-resolve-mcp)
- Remotion                      - optional, for motion graphics

No personal editing style is built in - neutral defaults only. Effects are used when you ask for them.

HOW TO INSTALL (10-15 minutes)
1. Unzip this folder somewhere you will keep it (e.g. Documents).
2. Open Claude Code (or any AI app with MCP support) in that folder.
3. Open setup/SETUP-PROMPT.txt, copy all of it, paste it in, press Enter.
4. Say which software you use. The assistant guides you step by step and checks each step.
5. Start a new session and test: "ultra edit this video" with a short recording.

WHAT YOU NEED
- An AI assistant with MCP support (Claude Code / Claude Desktop recommended)
- Your editor: Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve (free is fine) and/or After Effects
- Node.js 18+, Python 3.10-3.12, ffmpeg (the setup installs what is missing)
- Your own music and footage
